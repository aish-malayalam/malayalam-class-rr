Possessive Case

July 26, 2026

What is the Possessive Case?

In English, we add 's to show ownership.

Examples:

  • John's book
  • Sarah's pencil

In Malayalam, the ending of the word changes instead, often using sounds such as nde or de.

Possessive Pronouns

English Malayalam Pronunciation
My ende
Your (nee) ninde
Your (ningal) ningalde
His avande / addehathinde
Her avalde / avarde
Their avarde
Our (includes listener) nammalde
Our (excludes listener) njangalde

Grammar Rules

Rule 1
Words ending in a / i / e / r / l
Add de.

Rule 2
Words ending in m
Remove m and add thinde.

Rule 3
All other sounds
Add nde.

Challenge Question

Your friend comes to your house.

How would you say our house?

  • nammalde house
  • njangalde house

Think before opening the answer.

⭐ Reveal the answer

Since your friend is included, say nammalde house.
