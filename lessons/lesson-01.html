Lesson 1

Pronouns

July 25, 2026

Class Rule

Practice every day.

Pronouns

English Malayalam pronunciation When to use it
I njaan Talking about yourself
You nee Younger person or someone close to you
You ningal Older person or respectful form
He avan Younger person
He addeham Older person or respectful form
She aval Younger person
She avar Older person or respectful form
They avar Talking about more than one person
We nammal Includes the person you are speaking to
We njangal Does not include the person you are speaking to

This and That

This

ithu

That

athu

This before a noun

ee

That before a noun

aa

Examples

ithu — this

athu — that

ee book — this book

aa book — that book

Practice

  1. Say every pronoun aloud three times.
  2. Practice the difference between nee and ningal.
  3. Practice the difference between nammal and njangal.
  4. Point to nearby and faraway objects and say ithu or athu.
